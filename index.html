HOW IT WORKS

Separate the goal from the investment assumptions.

First, Carrowmont estimates what the goal may cost at the selected date. Then it projects existing savings, monthly contributions and any future lump sum using the investment-return assumption. The difference becomes the modelled funding gap.

For home purchase, the target is the selected down payment. For emergency savings, the target is based on essential monthly expenses and the number of months selected.

METHODOLOGY

Transparent calculations.

Future goal cost: today’s goal cost × (1 + inflation assumption)years.

Existing savings: projected at the entered annual investment return. Monthly contributions are modelled as end-of-month contributions using the monthly rate implied by the annual return.

Required monthly amount: the monthly contribution that would model to the future target after allowing for existing savings and eligible future lump sums.

All assumptions are user-controlled and constant for modelling purposes. Actual inflation, investment returns, taxes and future prices may differ materially.
